# AI Customer Churn Prediction

## Project Overview

An end-to-end machine learning project that predicts customer churn using telecommunications customer data.

The project combines Python, machine learning, explainable AI (SHAP), and Power BI to analyze customer behavior, predict churn probabilities, and identify high-risk customers.

## Dataset

**Telco Customer Churn Dataset**

- Total Customers: 7,043
- Churned Customers: 1,869
- Retained Customers: 5,174
- Churn Rate: 26.54%

7. SHAP explainability
8. Interactive Power BI dashboard development

## Power BI Dashboards

### 1. Executive Overview

Analyzes total customers, churn rate, customer retention, contract types, and payment methods.

### 2. Customer Segmentation

Analyzes customer churn across tenure groups, internet services, billing preferences, and demographic segments.

### 3. AI Customer Risk Analysis

Displays predicted churn, high-risk customer segments, churn probabilities, and customer-level predictions.

## Key Insights

- Approximately 26.54% of customers in the dataset have churned.
- Month-to-month contract customers show higher churn rates than customers on longer contracts.
- Fiber optic customers have higher observed churn rates than DSL customers.
- AI predictions help identify customers for further retention analysis.
